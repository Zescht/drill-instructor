    Record samples

    Four short recordings of your gun and your shooting place. They let Claude teach the app what your shot sounds like, and what is not a shot. Recordings are kept on this phone until you delete them.

    Before you start: how to make good recordings
    • Real conditions: record at the place where you will shoot, with the same rifle, target and distance.
    • Same microphone as in drills: connect and wear your AirPods before pressing Record, exactly as during a drill. The phone stays where it will be during drills (pocket, pouch, table).
    • Don't touch the phone while recording, and don't cover the AirPods.
    • Silence at both ends: wait 2 seconds after pressing Record before the first sound, and 2 seconds after the last sound before pressing Stop.
    • Full gas and full magazine. A gun low on gas sounds different. If you had to refill during a recording, mention it in the note.
    • Count your shots and enter the number after the recording. That number is the "answer key" for the tuning.
    • One recording per step. If something goes wrong, press Redo.
    Set the iPhone Mic Mode to "Wide Spectrum"
    1. Tap Turn on microphone below (Mic Mode can only be changed while the mic is on).
    2. Swipe down from the top-right corner to open Control Center.
    3. Tap Mic Mode at the top and choose Wide Spectrum. If Wide Spectrum is greyed out, choose Automatic. Never Voice Isolation.
    4. Come back here and select below what you chose. The iPhone keeps this choice for this app.

    Why: "Voice Isolation" blocks everything that is not a voice, including your shots. "Wide Spectrum" leaves all sounds in. "Automatic" is the next best. Wide Spectrum is greyed out in some situations (for example, iOS may treat a browser microphone like a call). A website cannot read this setting, so the app asks you, and your answer is sent along with the recordings.

    Send to Claude

    1. Tap Create upload key. GitHub opens (log in as Zescht if asked). Name, expiry (1 year) and the permission are already filled in.
    2. Under Repository access choose Only select repositories and pick drill-samples.
    3. Check that Contents says Read and write. Scroll down and tap Generate token.
    4. Copy the key (it starts with github_pat_), come back to this page, paste it below and tap Save key.
